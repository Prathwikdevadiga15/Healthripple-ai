The Challenge

Healthcare in India:
Three Critical Gaps

Millions lack health awareness, continuous care support, and emergency access — especially where language barriers, limited connectivity, and scarcity of professionals create life-threatening delays.

01

Lack of Early Awareness

People ignore symptoms due to lack of accessible health information in their native language. Preventable diseases escalate to critical stages.

67%of rural patients delay seeking care
02

Fragmented Care Support

Medication reminders, follow-up care, and health education are disconnected. Patients forget prescriptions. Caregivers are overwhelmed.

50%of patients miss follow-up medications
03

Emergency Access Gap

In emergencies, people don't know the nearest facility, available ambulances, or what first aid to provide. Precious minutes are lost.

45 minavg emergency response in rural areas
